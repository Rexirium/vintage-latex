% A dependency-free three-line drop capital. The following word is supplied
% separately so it can be set in small capitals.
\newcommand{\dropcap}[2]{%
  \noindent
  \parshape 4
    3.55em \dimexpr\linewidth-3.55em\relax
    3.55em \dimexpr\linewidth-3.55em\relax
    3.55em \dimexpr\linewidth-3.55em\relax
    0pt \linewidth
  \makebox[0pt][r]{%
    \raisebox{-1.48\baselineskip}[0pt][0pt]{%
      \fontsize{47}{47}\selectfont #1}%
    \hspace{0.16em}}%
  \textsc{#2}%
}

\dropcap{A}{n experiment} was made in a darkened chamber, where a narrow
beam entered through a slit and fell upon a triangular glass. The image upon
the farther paper was obſerved to be neither circular nor still, but stretched
according to the inclination of the glass. The figures 3, 4, 5, 7, and 9 in
this sentence demonstrate old-style numerals.

\marginpar{\footnotesize\raggedright The long \emph{s} in
  \emph{obſerved} is explicit Unicode; the font's Historic feature supplies
  several others.}

From repeated trials it appeared that the several rays did not bend in equal
measure. The circumstance was therefore recorded apart from the conjecture:
first the position of the apparatus, then the distance to the receiving sheet,
and only afterward the proposed account of the effect.

The old-style figures, restrained ink color, narrow measure, broad outer
margin, spaced small capitals, marginal note, and printer's rules are separate
controls. Remove any one of them to judge its contribution rather than treating
``vintage'' as a single indivisible style.
